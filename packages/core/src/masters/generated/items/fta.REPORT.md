## Slot decisions

The ITEMS sheet has two places for a preferential notification: `Basic_Notn` and `SAPTA_Notn`. The rule used: a notification that exempts duty "in excess of the amount calculated at the rate specified" (an effective rate) or exempts the whole duty files in the Basic slot; a notification that exempts "so much of that portion of the applied rate of duty as is specified" (a percentage remitted) files in the SAPTA slot, and so does every notification of a scheme built that way. Golden ex_job2 confirms DFTP in SAPTA even for its whole-duty clause (i).

- BASIC: Japan, Korea (152/2009 rates, 151/2009 whole duty), AIFTA, Singapore 10/2008 and 73/2005, Malaysia, Thailand EHS (whole duty since 86/2006), UAE, Australia, Mauritius, Oman, UK, EFTA, SAFTA (68/2012 rates, 99/2011 whole duty), Nepal, Bhutan.
- SAPTA: APTA, ISFTA, DFTP, Chile, MERCOSUR, Afghanistan (all "portion of the applied rate").
- Mixed, marked per line with `slot: "SAPTA"`: Singapore 74/2005 and 75/2005 (duty in excess of 90%/95% of the applied rate — 10%/5% remitted, `rate` 10/5), Australia TABLE III, Mauritius TABLE 2. The consumer reads `slot` per agreement only; these lines need it read per line.

## Reading the schedule files

- Every `<KEY>.json` is a plain array. Each line: `serial` (as printed; `(i)` for DFTP clause (i); empty for 99/2011, which prints none), `include`/`exclude` (digit prefixes, ranges expanded against the union of the CBIC 2024 tariff index and the 2026-27 tariff book, then compressed), `anyChapter` only on whole-tariff clauses (DFTP (i), SAFTA-LDC 99/2011, ISFTA LIST 5) and on the description-only Nepal/Bhutan lines, `description`, `rateText`, `rate`, `validFrom`, `validUntil` (always null — no sunset was machine-read; Korea bilateral-safeguard provisos are listed below as unapplied), `page` (null for rows inserted by an amendment), `notification`.
- Extra fields: `table` (TABLE I/II/III, APPENDIX I, PART A, LIST 3…), `specText` (column (2) as printed), `allGoods`, `aidcRate`/`healthCessRate`/`trqText`/`conditions` where the table has them, `slot` on SAPTA-type lines inside a BASIC agreement, `amendedBy`, `unappliedAmendments`, `specErratum`.
- Lines excluded by the notification itself (DFTP Appendix II, 99/2011 ANNEXURE, 68/2012 TABLE III) are not emitted; their codes are in the `exclude` of the lines they carve out of.
- A row whose range no longer exists in today's tariff (e.g. AIFTA/Malaysia/Korea "090940 to 090950", pre-2022 HS) keeps an empty `include` and matches nothing; it is listed under "unread tariff specs".
- Specific-rate cells ("22.3% or Rs.26.9/- per kg whichever is lower", AU coking coal per tonne) keep `rateText` with `rate` null.
